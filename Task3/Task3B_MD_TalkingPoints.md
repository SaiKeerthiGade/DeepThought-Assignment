---

## Key Insight (Beyond KPIs)
The issue is not only ad efficiency.
Product listing quality (especially images) is limiting conversion rates.

Even with better traffic, conversion will not improve significantly unless listing quality improves.

---

## What is Actually Happening

- Client perception: “results not improving”

### 1. Improve Product Listings
- Upgrade images and creatives
- Expected impact: +10–20% CTR improvement
- Direct improvement in ROAS without increasing ad spend

### 2. Focus on High-Intent Keywords
- Reduce broad match waste
- Improve conversion efficiency

### 3. Expand to Flipkart
- Existing store not utilized for ads
- Opportunity for additional revenue stream

### 4. Sponsored Display Expansion
- Move beyond search ads into awareness + retargeting
