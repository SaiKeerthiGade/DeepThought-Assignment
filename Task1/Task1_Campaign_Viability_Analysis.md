# Task 1: Campaign Viability Analysis

## Brand Details

| Field | Value |
|---------|---------|
| Product | Premium Face Serum |
| Selling Price (SP) | ₹800 |
| COGS | ₹280 |
| Amazon Referral Fee | 12% |
| Monthly Ad Spend | ₹3,00,000 |
| Units Sold via Ads | 1,500 |
| Organic Units Sold | 2,000 |
| Fixed Costs | ₹1,50,000 |

---

# Q1. Unit Economics

### Gross Margin

Gross Margin = SP - COGS

= 800 - 280

= ₹520

### Amazon Fee

Amazon Fee = 12% × 800

= ₹96

### Ad Cost Per Unit

Ad Cost Per Unit = 3,00,000 ÷ 1,500

= ₹200

### Net Margin Per Ad Unit

Net Margin = Gross Margin - Amazon Fee - Ad Cost

= 520 - 96 - 200

= ₹224

---

# Q2. Monthly Profitability

### Organic Margin Per Unit

= 520 - 96

= ₹424

### Profit from Ad Sales

= 224 × 1,500

= ₹3,36,000

### Profit from Organic Sales

= 424 × 2,000

= ₹8,48,000

### Total Profit Before Fixed Cost

= 3,36,000 + 8,48,000

= ₹11,84,000

### Monthly Profit

= 11,84,000 - 1,50,000

= ₹10,34,000

---

# Q3. ACOS and TACOS

### Ad Revenue

= 800 × 1,500

= ₹12,00,000

### ACOS

= 3,00,000 ÷ 12,00,000 × 100

= 25%

### Total Revenue

= 800 × (1,500 + 2,000)

= ₹28,00,000

### TACOS

= 3,00,000 ÷ 28,00,000 × 100

= 10.71%

### Interpretation

ACOS of 25% is healthy because advertising cost is manageable.

TACOS of 10.71% indicates strong overall business health due to significant organic sales.

---

# Q4. Break-Even Ad Spend

Let Monthly Profit = 0

Maximum Affordable Ad Spend

= Total Gross Contribution - Fixed Costs

= ₹13,34,000

Break-even ACOS

= 13,34,000 ÷ 12,00,000 × 100

≈ 111.17%

This is the theoretical limit before the business becomes unprofitable.

---

# Q5. Scenario: ACOS Worsens to 40%

Ad Revenue = 12,00,000

New Ad Spend

= 40% × 12,00,000

= ₹4,80,000

New Ad Units = 1,200

Ad Cost Per Unit

= 4,80,000 ÷ 1,200

= ₹400

Net Margin

= 520 - 96 - 400

= ₹24

Ad Profit

= 24 × 1,200

= ₹28,800

Organic Profit

= 424 × 2,000

= ₹8,48,000

Total Profit

= 28,800 + 8,48,000 - 1,50,000

= ₹7,26,800

### Recommendation

Campaign remains profitable but ad efficiency has dropped significantly. Keyword optimization, listing improvement, and bid control are recommended.

---

# Q6. Scenario: Double Ad Spend

New Ad Spend = ₹6,00,000

Ad Units = 2,500

Ad Cost Per Unit

= 6,00,000 ÷ 2,500

= ₹240

Net Margin

= 520 - 96 - 240

= ₹184

Ad Profit

= 184 × 2,500

= ₹4,60,000

Organic Profit

= ₹8,48,000

Total Profit

= 4,60,000 + 8,48,000 - 1,50,000

= ₹11,58,000


### Conclusion

Profit increases from ₹10,34,000 to ₹11,58,000.

Although returns show diminishing efficiency, scaling is still beneficial because total profit grows.
